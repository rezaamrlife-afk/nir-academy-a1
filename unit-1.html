Lesson 1A

A cappuccino, please

Meet Helen, Tom and the barista in four café conversations.

The story: A cappuccino, please

Listening

Listening 1.2

Conversation 1 — A cappuccino, please

Barista 1: What's your name?

Helen: Helen.

Barista 1: Ellen?

Helen: No, Helen.

Barista 1: Helen. OK.

Helen: A cappuccino, please.

Conversation 2 — Your cappuccino

Barista 2: Are you Diana?

Helen: No, I'm not. I'm Helen.

Barista 2: Sorry.

Barista: Helen? Your cappuccino.

Helen: Thanks.

Conversation 3 — Nice to meet you

Tom: Hello. Are you Helen?

Helen: Yes, I am. And you're Tom.

Tom: Yes! Nice to meet you.

Helen: Nice to meet you.

Tom: Just a minute.

Conversation 4 — A tea, please

Tom: Hi. A tea, please.

Barista 1: What's your name?

Tom: Tom.

Barista 1: Dom. A tea.

Tom: No, I'm Tom, not Dom.

N NIR Academy ENGLISH GRAMMAR
A1 BEGINNER · GRAMMAR GUIDE

Verb be: I and you

Use am with I and are with you.

01

I + am

I am ...

EXAMPLE

I am a student.

I'm a student.

I'm is the short form of I am.

02

You + are

You are ...

EXAMPLE

You are a student.

You're a student.

You're is the short form of you are.

03 Ask a question

A: Are you Helen?

B: Yes, I am.

B: No, I'm not.

Put Are before you to make a question.

04 Remember!

✓ I am / I'm Correct
✓ You are / You're Correct
× I are Incorrect
× You am Incorrect
✦

Hint for exercise a

Look carefully at the picture. Use I when you speak about yourself. Use you when you speak to another person.
