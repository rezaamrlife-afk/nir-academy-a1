Tom: Yes! Nice to meet you.

Helen: Nice to meet you.

Tom: Just a minute.

Conversation 4 — A tea, please

Tom: Hi. A tea, please.

Barista 1: What's your name?

Tom: Tom.

Barista 1: Dom. A tea.

Tom: No, I'm Tom, not Dom.

N NIR Academy ENGLISH GRAMMAR
A1 BEGINNER · GRAMMAR GUIDE

Verb be: I and you

Use am with I and are with you.

01

I + am

I am ...

EXAMPLE

I am a student.

I'm a student.

I'm is the short form of I am.

02

You + are

You are ...

EXAMPLE

You are a student.

You're a student.

You're is the short form of you are.

03 Ask a question

A: Are you Helen?

B: Yes, I am.

B: No, I'm not.

Put Are before you to make a question.

04 Remember!

✓ I am / I'm Correct
✓ You are / You're Correct
× I are Incorrect
× You am Incorrect
✦

Hint for exercise a

Look carefully at the picture. Use I when you speak about yourself. Use you when you speak to another person.

N NIR Academy ENGLISH GRAMMAR
A1 BEGINNER · GRAMMAR GUIDE

Verb be: he, she and it

Use is with he, she and it.

01

He + is

He is ...

EXAMPLE

He is a student.

He's a student.

He is for a man or a boy. He's is the short form of he is.

02

She + is

She is ...

EXAMPLE

She is from Spain.

She's from Spain.

She is for a woman or a girl. She's is the short form of she is.

03

It + is

It is ...

EXAMPLE

Lima is in Peru. It is a big city.

It's a big city.

It is for a thing or a place. It's is the short form of it is.

04 Ask a question

A: Is she from Brazil?

B: Yes, she is.

B: No, she isn't.

Put Is before he, she or it to make a question.

05 Remember!

✓ He is / He's Correct
✓ She is / She's Correct
✓ It is / It's Correct
× He are Incorrect
× She am Incorrect
✦

Hint for exercise a

Use he for a man, she for a woman and it for a thing or a place.
